A letter for you

Tap the envelope to open it.

Dear Sughanda,

Some people walk into a room and it quietly turns into a garden. You do that. You carry softness and shine wherever you go, and the world is a little warmer for it.

You find words for feelings other people only feel, and you make the ordinary look like roses. I hope you never stop seeing the world the way you do.

May this new year bring you slow mornings, kind surprises, and laughter that squeezes your eyes shut, the way it does when you’re truly happy. May every dream you’ve been tucking away find its sunlight.

Today, be celebrated, be spoiled, be loved out loud. You deserve a life as beautiful as you are.

Happy Birthday, beautiful.
Keep blooming.

Janamdin mubarak, Sughanda
